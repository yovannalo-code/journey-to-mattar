The road to Mattar

Your 13 stops, in the order the flock walks them. A group reaches a new stop about every week and a half and arrives at the Tannery in about 4 to 5 months. The Tannery is a new draft, based on your copper-fin tower.

    Nothing here is in the game yet. Once you're happy with the Tannery, these stops go into the prototype.
