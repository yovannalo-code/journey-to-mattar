Privacy

Journey to Mattar · last updated 19 October 2026

Journey to Mattar is a small group game. Each action you log (a saving, a tithe or an initiative) moves your group's sheep one step along a map. This page explains what the game stores and who can see it.

What we store

  • From your Google account: your name, email address and profile picture, used only to sign you in and to show your name to people in your journeys.
  • Your journeys: the journeys you start or join, and when you joined.
  • Your steps: the type of each step (savings, tithe or initiative), the optional note you write, and when you logged it. No amounts of money are recorded.

Who can see it

Only people in the same journey can see that journey, its members and its steps. Journeys never see each other. We don't sell or share your information, and there are no ads or trackers.

Where it's kept

Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts the game's database and handles Google sign-in. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Removing your data

You can remove any of your own steps in the game (tap a footprint or open Activity). To have your account and everything linked to it deleted, ask the person who runs your group, or open a request at github.com/yovannalo-code/journey-to-mattar/issues.
